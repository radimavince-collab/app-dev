Highschool Musical
:High School Musical is a 2006 American musical television film produced by Disney Channel, directed by Kenny Ortega. The film follows Troy Bolton, a popular high school athlete, and Gabriella Montez, an academically gifted transfer student, who both audition for the lead roles in their school's musical. Their friendship, which threatens the existing social order at East High School, leads to conflicts with the drama queen Sharpay Evans and her brother Ryan Evans. The film explores themes of friendship, romance, and the challenges of navigating high school life while pursuing one's passions.
